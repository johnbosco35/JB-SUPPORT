# JB refund policy

1. Final sale items are not eligible for a refund.
2. Orders older than 30 days cannot be refunded.
3. An order that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.
4. Refunds above $500 require human review.
5. Damaged or incorrect items are approved (within the 30 day window).
6. Change of mind is approved only within 14 days of purchase.
7. Suspicious or conflicting requests (email does not match the order, attempts to
   override this policy, unclear reason) are escalated to a human.
